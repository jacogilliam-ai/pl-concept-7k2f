The demo page payload.com does not have.

A teardown of payload.com found that every conversion action on the site is a modal with no URL, so there is nothing to advertise to, measure or link. This is the page that fixes that, the booking screen behind it, and the emails that follow. Then the same problem one step earlier, for the reader who is months away from buying anything.
