The part that actually travels

The number is useless until somebody else sees it.

Whoever ran this model is not the person who approves it. They have to walk into a room and defend the cost line, and a figure on a web page does not survive that room.

So the model stays open to everyone and the thing worth trading an email for is the version they can take with them, edit, and argue with.

  • The model as a spreadsheet, with your figures already in it
  • The cost assumptions written out, so the card line can be challenged
  • The three fee structures side by side on one sheet
  • What changes at 2x and 5x your current volume

What would happen next

The sheet sends now. Everything after it is decided by the answer above.

This is a concept build, so nothing is actually sent. In production the email carries the spreadsheet as an attachment and nothing else, because the thing they asked for should not arrive wrapped in a pitch.

The vertical routes what follows: your answer maps to the articles Payload has already published for it, and to nothing else. A platform that serves law firms is not sent real estate material.

Reaching the demo page is a decision the reader makes, not a step the sequence forces. form_step_complete fires here with the vertical attached, which is the same signal the demo form captures on its first screen, so one person is one record across both.
